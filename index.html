    FAQ

    Questions people always ask

    Are the prices on this site live?
    No. Every figure here is a planning estimate to help you compare options. Component prices and stock change constantly, so the links open live Amazon Spain listings where you can confirm the real price before ordering.
    How do I know if my parts are compatible?
    Three things cause most failed builds: a CPU that does not fit the motherboard socket, memory of the wrong generation (DDR4 vs DDR5), and an undersized power supply. Use the compatibility checker above — it validates all three and recommends a wattage with safe headroom.
    What power supply wattage do I need?
    Add the graphics card and processor power draw, add roughly 90 W for the rest of the system, then apply about 40 % headroom for transient spikes and future upgrades. A modern 750 W 80+ Gold unit covers most 1440p builds, while 4K-class cards want 850 W or more.
    Should I upgrade my CPU or my GPU first?
    Check which component sits at 100 % while gaming. High GPU usage with low CPU usage means the graphics card is the limit; the reverse points to a CPU bottleneck. On most older machines a fast NVMe drive plus more memory gives the biggest everyday improvement per euro.
    Do you buy the parts for me?
    Send the plan you generated and the parts you are considering, and you will get confirmation of what is compatible and what to avoid. Any purchase is always made in your name, in your own account, with your own payment method.
    Do I need an account to send a request?
    No — you can send a request straight away as a guest. An account is optional, and it simply keeps your requests together so you can look them up later. Creating one takes an email address and a password, and if you ever forget the password you can reset it from a link on the sign-in form.
    Is my information stored on this website?
    Your request is delivered straight to GameFix Hub and stored so it cannot be lost, and you get a reference number to quote back. If the automatic delivery is ever unavailable, the form falls back to preparing a message in your browser for you to send by email or WhatsApp instead. Your saved list and form draft stay in this browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
